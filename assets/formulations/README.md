# Teaching figures

The [problem formulations](../../README.md#problem-formulations) embed these figures alongside the corresponding formulations. The Benders charts show the recorded master lower bound, best feasible integer solution cost, and cuts generated in each iteration. The column-generation charts show the restricted master LP objective, best reported pricing reduced cost, and available column count. These are reference-solver iterations; coding-agent attempts are recorded separately.

The charts visualize the saved JSON records listed above. For FCTP, early recorded bounds are infinite while recourse is infeasible; the bound panel displays the phase with finite values, while the cut panel includes every iteration. The original archived FCTP progress PDF has 213 iterations; the canonical reference trace used here has 207. The chart and its caption use the canonical trace consistently.

Line colors use the dark blue and green entries of the colorblind-safe four-class ColorBrewer Paired palette: `#1f78b4` and `#33a02c`. The full four-class palette is `#a6cee3`, `#1f78b4`, `#b2df8a`, and `#33a02c`. Distinct line styles, marker shapes, and legends also identify the plotted roles. Palette specifications are by Cynthia Brewer and colleagues; see [ColorBrewer](https://colorbrewer2.org/).

The two FCTP network pictures, `fctp_instance.png` and `fctp_solution.png`, are raster renderings of the existing `instance.pdf` and `solution_bd.pdf` in the archived `z_artifacts/benchmarks/bd/fctp/fctp_large_seed000/plots/` folder. They preserve the source figures' colors and geometry. Their supply, demand, fixed costs, and capacities match the [canonical instance](../../data/bd/fctp/fctp_large_seed000/data.json); unit costs differ only at floating-point rounding precision. The repository keeps [the instance PNG](fctp_instance.png) and [the solution PNG](fctp_solution.png); local plot PDFs under data/ are regenerated by the generators and ignored by Git.
