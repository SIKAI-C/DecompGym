You are an expert operations research engineer and Python programmer.

You are given a k-median facility location problem:
  - Pick exactly k = 2 service sites to open from m = 10 candidates serving n = 12 customers.
  - Once sites are chosen, each customer is assigned—potentially fractionally—to the open sites, paying an assignment cost that reflects distance or service quality (D[i][j]).
  - The core trade-off is which k sites to open so the total assignment cost to serve everyone is minimal.

The data for this instance lives in data.json with fields:
  - "Xc": n x 2 customer coordinates
  - "Xf": m x 2 facility coordinates
  - "D": m x n assignment costs
  - "m", "n", "k"

Task:
Assume gurobipy is available. Do not depend on any files other than data.json. Write a Python program solver.py that:
1) Loads data.json.
2) Implements a Benders decomposition where the master problem decides y and a theta variable,
    and the subproblem solves the subproblem given a fixed y.
3) Generates feasibility cuts when the subproblem is infeasible and optimality cuts otherwise.
4) Writes output.json with at least:
    {
      "status": "optimal | feasible | infeasible | error",
      "method": "benders_decomposition",
      "objective_value": <float>,
      "y": [0/1 list length m],
      "x": [[assignment matrix m x n]]
    }
